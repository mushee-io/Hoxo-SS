What must happen first

Critical requirement

Verified on-chain engineering

Complete canonical oracle and risk authority, integrated margin, deployed audited source/WASM provenance and a genuine multi-wallet Testnet financial lifecycle.

Critical requirement

Independent audit and response

External code and economic review, fully remediated critical/high findings, contract-level emergency pause drills, recovery procedures and verified guardian permissions.

Critical requirement

Liquidity, insurance and risk

Proven capital sources, market-maker arrangements, insurance reserves, signed leverage/collateral limits and a funded loss response. No fictional TVL or public trading volume.

Critical requirement

Governance and legal authorisation

Reviewed multisig key controls, legal/regulatory advice, disclosed customer risks, final source-specific change control and an explicit independently signed launch decision.
